# Chord Sovereigns V8

**Goal:** Audit the actual deployed four worlds, tablet input and performance, then give every final encounter a distinctive musical sovereign.

**Scope:** Existing hybrid sprite/Canvas/pixelfx architecture. Four new boss atlases, three readable boss phases, beat-synchronized telegraph/cast/shield-break/defeat visuals. Preserve the five-key/touch scheme, audio clock, educational flow and ordinary enemy encounters. No dodging, surprise extra damage, new songs or paid services.

## Direction

- Theater: phantom maestro with organ crown and burgundy/gold cape; orchestral staff barrages and broken chord seal.
- Rooftop: monumental turntable DJ with stereo speaker towers; equalizer shockwaves and bass-ring charge.
- Orbit: celestial synth conductor with orbital halo and keyboard armor; constellation arrays and rail-chord release.
- Fantasy: moonlit harp sovereign in a branch crown and star mantle; resonant strings and luminous star blossoms.

## Design Rulings

- The boss casts musical patterns on the adventure half only. Existing misses remain the sole harmony penalty; no hidden second penalty or extra control is introduced for schoolchildren.
- Boss HP should scale conservatively with collectible notes in the final segment rather than song density making one boss trivial and another impossible. Exploration gets the gentler budget. The budget and damage remain independently testable.
- Changes will be committed/deployed only after validation, to the already authorized existing GitHub/Vercel target; preserve the remote visit counter.
- Freeze the boss budget on entry using remaining live note completions and the player's earlier judged damage. Target the playable finale rather than multiplying total notes by a fixed HP factor. No minimum-90 or maximum-2400 HP clamps, time-based immunity, damage banking or clock changes. Synthetic charts without late notes cannot guarantee a late-song kill; tests cover all14 actual charts in both modes and both successful judgment grades.
- Quiz content refers to the directly verified 2022 MOE music curriculum, not a claim of complete coverage or a recording-specific analysis. Detailed sources, achievement-code mapping and limitations are in `docs/music-curriculum-v8.md`.
