# Musical Feedback V5

**Goal:** Five clearly distinct pixelfx feedback tiers plus a verifiable falling-bar reference analysis.

**Architecture:** Shared pure feedback profiles and a cached Aseprite sprite player serve both note and battle canvases. Judge events carry hold identity, combat resolves kill identity at actual impact. Effects follow song time; visual boss epilogue never delays or freezes input/music during play.

## Design

- Normal: small warm-gold hit spark, short recoil, no screen shake.
- Perfect: larger ice-white critical star and ring, stronger but brief reaction.
- Hold: cyan sustained rune circles, traveling tail marks, distinct completion zap.
- Combo: magenta mana burst with musical chord and actual combo count, limited battle-only shake.
- Boss: large star/poof dissolution, final chord and victory text; retain enough visual time before results for a last-note kill.
- Notes and judgment line never shake. Reduced-motion disables shake and dynamic hold rotation; effects are bounded and one-shots end empty.
